    Sensors measure crank angle, RPM, throttle, oxygen, and temperature. The ECU calculates load, fuel, and ignition timing. Actuators fire injectors, coils, idle valves, and fans.
    Real ECUs are more complex: they use tables, corrections, knock control, emissions logic, diagnostics, and safety checks. This simulator keeps the math simple so the control idea is visible.
